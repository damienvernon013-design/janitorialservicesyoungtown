How to Get an Accurate Janitorial Quote Without the Guesswork

A little preparation before the walkthrough gets you a quote that holds up once service starts.

Getting a janitorial quote is easy. Getting an accurate one takes a little preparation before the walkthrough. Facilities that consistently receive fair, reliable quotes share a few habits in common.

Know Your Square Footage

Cleaners price largely by the foot. The more accurate your number, the more accurate your quote. Walk the space yourself first, and know which areas are included versus excluded — storage rooms, server rooms, and unoccupied space matter.

Define Your Scope Before the Walkthrough

Write down what you need before a provider walks the building: which rooms, which tasks, how often, and your non-negotiables. This prevents a provider from quoting a stripped-down version of what you actually need.

Compare More Than One Quote

One quote gives you a number. A few quotes give you a market. Middle-range quotes are usually the sweet spot — the lowest bids often come with understaffing or hidden exclusions, and the highest bid is not automatically the best service.

Ask What’s Not Included

Always ask a provider to state plainly what falls outside their standard quote. This surfaces gaps before you sign, not three months in.

Ask for References From Similar Facilities

A provider that excels in retail may not be the right fit for a school or medical office. Ask for references from facilities of a similar size and type to yours.

What a Walkthrough With Us Looks Like

A supervisor walks your facility, documents every area and task, and delivers a written flat-rate quote within 48 hours — no hourly estimates, no ranges, one number tied to a scope you can hold us to.
