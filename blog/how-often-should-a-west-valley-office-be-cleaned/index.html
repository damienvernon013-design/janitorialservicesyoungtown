How Often Should a West Valley Office Be Professionally Cleaned?

The right frequency depends on headcount, visitor traffic, and facility type — not a flat rule.

This is one of the most common questions from facilities shopping for janitorial service, and the answer depends on several factors specific to your building.

The General Rule

For most office environments, two to three cleaning visits per week strikes the right balance between hygiene, cost, and actual need. High-traffic offices with more than 20 employees, or spaces with regular public visitors, typically need daily service.

Factors That Determine Frequency

  • Headcount: more people means more high-touch surfaces and restroom usage.
  • Visitor traffic: if the public visits regularly, cleanliness is tied directly to how your organization is perceived.
  • Facility type: medical offices and schools generally warrant daily service by industry norm.
  • Layout: open-plan spaces with shared surfaces accumulate grime faster than private offices.
  • Season: cold and flu season may warrant temporarily increasing frequency.

Frequency by Facility Type

  • Small professional office (under 10 people): 1–2x per week
  • Mid-size office (10–30 people): 2–3x per week
  • Large open-plan office (30+): nightly
  • Medical or dental office: nightly, plus daytime protocols between patients
  • School or municipal building: nightly, with day porter coverage during high-occupancy hours
  • Warehouse or light industrial: 2–3x per week, more often in high-traffic zones

Don’t Skip Periodic Deep Cleans

Regardless of your regular frequency, schedule periodic deep cleans for carpets, floor waxing, and hard-to-reach areas. Most providers offer these as scheduled add-ons at a favorable rate for existing clients.
