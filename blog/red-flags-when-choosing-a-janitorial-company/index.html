Red Flags to Watch for When Choosing a Janitorial Company

Most providers present well in a sales conversation. The gaps show up later — unless you know what to check first.

Most janitorial companies present well in a sales conversation. The gaps usually show up later — in inconsistent service, surprise charges, or a different crew every few weeks. Here is what to check before you sign.

Red Flags Before You Sign

  • No written scope of work: a verbal-only quote with no task list leaves no accountability.
  • Unusually low pricing: a quote well below the range you’ve received elsewhere usually means something is missing.
  • No proof of insurance: a legitimate company can produce a current certificate of insurance within a day. Hesitation is a signal.
  • Vague answers about staffing: if they can’t explain whether staff are employees or subcontractors, proceed carefully.
  • High-pressure sales tactics: artificial urgency is a manipulation tactic, not a sign of a reputable business.
  • No references available: a company with legitimate commercial accounts should provide a few references.

Red Flags During the Relationship

  • Constant staff turnover: a different crew every few weeks means no institutional knowledge of your space.
  • Ignored complaints: slow or dismissive responses to valid complaints will not improve over time.
  • Skipped scope items: if tasks you’re paying for are regularly missed, document them and raise the issue formally.
  • Surprise invoice charges: additions not agreed to in writing signal billing practices that will escalate.
  • Reluctance to walk the space with you: a transparent provider welcomes periodic walkthroughs to review quality together.
