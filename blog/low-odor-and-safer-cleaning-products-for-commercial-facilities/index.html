Low-Odor and Safer Cleaning Products for Commercial Facilities

Product choice affects indoor air quality and employee comfort more than most facilities realize.

The cleaning industry uses a wide range of chemical products, and product choice has a direct effect on indoor air quality, employee comfort, and facilities with sensitive populations — schools, medical offices, and buildings with staff who have chemical sensitivities.

Why Product Selection Matters

Some conventional cleaning products release compounds that contribute to indoor air quality complaints — headaches, respiratory irritation, and general discomfort that track with strong-odor products used in enclosed spaces. Low-odor, appropriately labeled products reduce this exposure significantly.

What to Look For

  • Low-odor or fragrance-free formulations where appropriate for the space
  • Products suited to the specific facility type — medical-grade disinfectants for clinical settings, school-appropriate products for classrooms
  • Products that don’t require extended ventilation before a space can be reoccupied

Business Benefits

  • Employee comfort: fewer complaints related to strong chemical odors after cleaning
  • Appropriate for sensitive facilities: schools and medical offices benefit from a more deliberate product standard
  • Reduced liability: fewer harsh chemicals reduces the risk of chemical-related incidents

Ask Your Provider

When evaluating a janitorial proposal, ask which products are used in which areas and why. A provider that can answer specifically — rather than a generic “we use commercial-grade cleaners” — is more likely to have thought through product selection deliberately.
