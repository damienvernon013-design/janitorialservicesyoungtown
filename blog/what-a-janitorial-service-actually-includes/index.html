What Does a Commercial Janitorial Service Actually Include?

Standard service varies more between providers than most facilities expect. Here is what to check for.

Not all janitorial services are the same, and what’s included in a quote varies between providers. Before you sign a contract, know exactly what tasks are — and are not — part of your standard service.

Standard Inclusions in Most Janitorial Contracts

  • Vacuuming: all carpeted areas, including under desks and in corners
  • Hard floor care: sweeping and mopping tile, VCT, and sealed concrete
  • Trash removal: all bins emptied and relined
  • Restroom cleaning: toilets, sinks, mirrors, and floors scrubbed and disinfected; consumables restocked
  • Break room: counters, sink, and microwave exterior
  • Surface dusting: desks, shelves, and window ledges
  • Glass and mirrors: interior glass and partitions

Common Add-Ons Priced Separately

  • Carpet extraction
  • Floor stripping, waxing, and buffing
  • Exterior window washing
  • High dusting above reach height
  • Post-construction or event cleanup
  • Consumables restocking package (paper, soap, liners) if not already bundled

Ask Your Provider to Walk You Through It

Request a written scope of work rather than a verbal agreement. This protects you if quality falls short and gives you a clear basis for evaluating performance.

Customization Should Be Standard

If you have a server room that should never be entered, or specific equipment in a break room, tell your provider before the contract is written. A well-structured scope reflects your actual facility, not a generic template.
