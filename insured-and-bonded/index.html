Insured, Bonded and 22 Years Serving Youngtown

What our insurance coverage means for your facility, and how to request documentation.

When a contractor is working in your building after hours, you need to know that if something goes wrong, the liability lands on the contractor — not on you, and not on your facility's general liability policy. After 22 years in commercial cleaning, we carry the coverage that protects the buildings we work in.

General Liability Insurance

We carry commercial general liability insurance appropriate for the type and scale of work we perform across the West Valley. This coverage applies to property damage or bodily injury caused by our crews while working in your facility. A certificate of insurance is provided with every written quote. If your procurement process requires your facility to be named as an additional insured on our policy, we provide that endorsement at no additional cost.

Workers' Compensation

Every employee who cleans your building is covered under our workers' compensation policy. This is not a contractor arrangement where the liability for an injured worker falls back on your facility. If a crew member is injured while working on your property, our workers' compensation policy is the applicable coverage — not yours.

We do not use 1099 subcontractors for cleaning work. The people in your building are our employees, and our policies cover them as such.

Janitorial Bond

We carry a janitorial services bond. Bonding provides coverage in the event of theft or dishonesty by a cleaning crew member while on your premises. This is separate from general liability and is specifically structured for cleaning contractors who have access to facilities outside normal business hours.

22 Years in Business

A contractor with 22 years of continuous operation has maintained those policies and renewed them without lapse for two decades. That continuity is itself a signal — contractors who carry inadequate coverage or who let policies lapse tend not to stay in business this long. Request our current certificate of insurance with your quote and verify it directly with the issuing agent if your procurement requires it.

Requesting Documentation

Certificate of insurance, additional insured endorsements, and bond documentation are available upon request. Include your request in the quote form or email ops@thequotemasters.com with the facility name and the documents required. We aim to return insurance documentation within one business day of a request.
