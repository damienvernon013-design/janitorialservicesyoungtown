Our Janitorial Services in Youngtown, AZ

Three core service lines for commercial, institutional, and light-industrial facilities.

Youngtown Janitorial Services runs three service lines built for the facilities types we work in most frequently across the West Valley: nightly contract cleaning for offices and schools, on-site day porter programs for high-traffic buildings, and periodic floor care for VCT and sealed concrete.

Each service is scoped in writing before work begins. There is no standard contract that gets revised at renewal — you get a scope document specific to your facility, and that document is what we are measured against.

Nightly Janitorial Service

After-hours cleaning for offices, schools, municipal buildings, and light-industrial spaces. A fixed crew with a written checklist and a supervisor who signs off before leaving the building.

Full details →

Day Porter & Restroom Service

On-site cleaning during business hours. High-traffic restrooms, lobbies, break rooms, conference rooms, and event cleanups. Available on an hourly basis or as a full-time on-site placement.

Full details →

Floor Stripping & Waxing

Commercial VCT strip-and-wax and floor refinishing for sealed concrete. Scheduled around your operating hours. Includes strip, clean, seal, and multi-coat commercial-grade finish application.

Full details →
