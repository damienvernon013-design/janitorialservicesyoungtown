About Waddell

Waddell is an unincorporated community in Maricopa County at the intersection of Litchfield Road and Camelback Road, primarily agricultural and semi-rural in character, with commercial activity concentrated along the Litchfield Road corridor connecting it to Surprise to the north and Goodyear to the south.

The Phoenix Raceway in nearby Avondale (accessible via Interstate 10 and Litchfield Road from Waddell) and surrounding event infrastructure have driven commercial and light-industrial development along the Litchfield corridor, including warehousing, logistics, and contractor staging facilities that require janitorial programs.

Waddell's proximity to Luke Air Force Base — approximately 5 miles southeast — means the area includes contractor facilities, support businesses, and aerospace-adjacent light-industrial operations along Camelback Road that represent a niche but consistent cleaning account type.
