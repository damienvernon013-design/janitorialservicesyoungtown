Nightly Janitorial Service in Surprise, AZ

Youngtown Janitorial Services provides nightly after-hours janitorial service to commercial and institutional facilities in Surprise. Surprise is approximately 15–20 minutes from our Youngtown base — within our standard 25-mile radius with no distance surcharge.

After-hours access, written scope, fixed crew, supervisor-signed checklist on every visit. The scope is written for your building specifically — not a generic checklist applied to every facility we clean.

Example: A 9,500 sq ft single-storey dental and orthodontic group practice on bell road

The practice operates 10 operatory stations across two wings, a shared sterilization room, a laboratory, a consultation suite, and a large waiting area. Nightly service cleans all clinical-adjacent surfaces in treatment areas, restrooms, the front desk and waiting area, and the break room. The sterilization room is cleaned to protocol — surfaces wiped, floor mopped — but the sterilization equipment itself is not touched. The scope is explicit on this boundary to avoid liability exposure.
