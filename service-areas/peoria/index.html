About Peoria

Peoria is the third-largest city in the West Valley with a population of over 175,000 and a substantial commercial base, including the P83 Entertainment District on 83rd Avenue, a concentration of medical and dental offices along Happy Valley Road, and significant light-industrial parks in the north Peoria area near Lake Pleasant Parkway.

The Peoria Sports Complex, home to spring training for the San Diego Padres and Seattle Mariners, operates year-round event and conference facilities that require cleaning between events, during training season (February–March), and for stadium maintenance throughout the year.

North Peoria's office and industrial parks along the I-17 corridor — including facilities in the area around Pinnacle Peak Road — house a mix of distribution operations, professional offices, and light manufacturing that represents a significant concentration of potential janitorial accounts.
