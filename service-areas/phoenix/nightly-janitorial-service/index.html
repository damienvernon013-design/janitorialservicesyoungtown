Nightly Janitorial Service in Phoenix, AZ

Youngtown Janitorial Services provides nightly after-hours janitorial service to commercial and institutional facilities in Phoenix. Phoenix is approximately 20–30 minutes from our Youngtown base — within our standard 25-mile radius with no distance surcharge.

After-hours access, written scope, fixed crew, supervisor-signed checklist on every visit. The scope is written for your building specifically — not a generic checklist applied to every facility we clean.

Example: A 12,000 sq ft class b office building in the deer valley corridor on 23rd avenue

Three-storey building with six tenant suites including a financial services firm, a staffing agency, a medical billing company, and two professional services firms. Common areas include a shared lobby, elevator, three restroom sets, and a ground-floor conference room available to all tenants. Nightly service five nights per week covers all common areas plus each tenant suite per individual lease agreements. The scope tracks each tenant suite separately with separate task lists and separate contact protocols.

How to Get a Quote for Your Phoenix Facility

Call (866) 958-8773 Mon–Fri 8:30am–5:00pm, or submit a quote request. A supervisor will schedule a walkthrough of your Phoenix facility and return a written scope and flat monthly price within 48 hours of the visit.
