What the 25-Mile Radius Covers

Youngtown sits at the center of the West Valley's industrial and commercial corridor. The 25-mile radius from our base takes in the US-60 and I-101 corridor through Glendale and Peoria, the Grand Avenue corridor through El Mirage and Surprise, the Bell Road retail and office strip through Sun City, and the northwest Phoenix commercial zones along Happy Valley Road and Deer Valley.

If your facility is along any of these corridors, we are a practical service provider — not a contractor commuting 45 minutes each way.
