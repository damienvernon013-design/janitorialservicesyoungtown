About El Mirage

El Mirage is a fast-growing Phoenix suburb with a population that has roughly doubled since 2000, driven by residential and light-industrial development along the Dysart Road and Thunderbird Road corridors.

The city's primary commercial zone runs along Dysart Road and includes a mix of medical offices, retail strip centers, and small professional suites that feed demand for contract janitorial service.

El Mirage City Hall and the El Mirage Community Center on El Mirage Road are among the larger public facilities in the city requiring ongoing cleaning contracts — the community center hosts regular events that require turnaround cleaning between uses.
