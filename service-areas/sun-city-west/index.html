About Sun City West

Sun City West is a second planned retirement community adjacent to original Sun City, developed starting in 1978 and home to approximately 25,000 residents, with its own recreation centers, clubs, and community facilities managed by the Sun City West Recreation Association.

The community has a high concentration of medical, dental, and specialist offices — particularly along RH Johnson Boulevard — serving the retirement-age population, including practices that require consistent cleaning to meet the sanitation expectations of health-care-adjacent environments.

The R.H. Johnson Recreation Center and associated facilities in Sun City West operate multiple meeting rooms, auditoriums, fitness areas, and craft studios that generate demand for multi-use facility cleaning programs with frequent restroom and common-area turnaround.
