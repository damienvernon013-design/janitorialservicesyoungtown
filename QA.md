**El Mirage:** (1) Population roughly doubled since 2000, fast-growth suburb. (2) Primary commercial zone on Dysart Road — medical, retail, professional. (3) El Mirage City Hall and Community Center on El Mirage Road — events, city services.

**Peoria:** (1) 175,000+ population, P83 Entertainment District on 83rd Avenue, medical corridor on Happy Valley Road, industrial parks near Lake Pleasant Parkway. (2) Peoria Sports Complex — spring training for Padres and Mariners, year-round event facilities. (3) North Peoria office/industrial corridor along I-17 near Pinnacle Peak Road.

**Surprise:** (1) Grew from under 10,000 to 145,000+ residents since 2000. (2) Surprise Stadium on Bullard Avenue — spring training for Royals and Rangers, year-round events. (3) Commercial cluster along Bell Road and Litchfield Road near Surprise Marketplace.

**Glendale:** (1) ~250,000 population, State Farm Stadium and Gila River Arena entertainment district. (2) Camelback Ranch on 99th Avenue — Dodgers and White Sox spring training. (3) Industrial/commercial corridors on 51st and 59th Avenues — warehouses, distribution, light manufacturing.

**Sun City West:** (1) Second planned retirement community, built from 1978, ~25,000 residents, managed by Sun City West Recreation Association. (2) High concentration of medical and specialist offices on RH Johnson Boulevard. (3) R.H. Johnson Recreation Center — meeting rooms, auditoriums, fitness areas, craft studios.

**Waddell:** (1) Unincorporated Maricopa County community at Litchfield Road and Camelback Road, commercial activity on Litchfield Road corridor. (2) Phoenix Raceway vicinity driving commercial/industrial development along Litchfield Road — warehousing, logistics, contractor staging. (3) Proximity to Luke Air Force Base (~5 miles SE) generates aerospace-adjacent light-industrial accounts on Camelback Road.

**Phoenix (NW):** (1) Service covers northwest Phoenix — north of I-10, west of I-17, including Deer Valley, Happy Valley, Norterra corridors. (2) Deer Valley Airport industrial/office corridor on 19th and 23rd Avenues — aviation support, light-industrial, professional offices.
