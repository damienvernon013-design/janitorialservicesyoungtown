Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through this website.

This policy explains how Youngtown Janitorial Services collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website, including our quote request form and contact channels. Last updated 2026-08-17.

Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request or contact form, we collect the information you provide: name, phone number, email address, zip code, facility type, approximate square footage, and any notes you include. We do not collect payment information through this website.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our forms is used only to prepare and follow up on your quote request — scheduling a callback, arranging a walkthrough, and providing a written scope and price. We do not sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Store Your Information

Form submissions are transmitted securely to our internal customer relationship system for handling by our office staff. We retain lead information only as long as necessary to respond to your request and, if you become a customer, to service your account.

Cookies and Analytics

This site may use standard analytics and advertising tools to understand how visitors use our pages and to measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns. This includes recording UTM campaign parameters (such as utm_source) when you arrive from a link or ad, so we know which channel to credit for a lead. No personally identifiable information is collected through this tracking on its own.

Your Choices

You are not required to submit any information through this website. You may always call us directly at (866) 958-8773 to request a quote or ask questions without submitting a web form.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to ops@thequotemasters.com or by phone at (866) 958-8773, Monday–Friday 8:30am–5:00pm.
